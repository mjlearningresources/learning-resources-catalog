# Plan my year

Paste the text below into Claude, ChatGPT or another AI assistant that can open web links. Fill in the three blanks first.

---

I teach GRADE (for example: grade 6) and I want a plan for SUBJECTS (for example: US history and science) over TIME SPAN (for example: a 36-week school year).

Use this catalog of classroom resources: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mjlearningresources/learning-resources-catalog/main/llms.txt

Steps:
1. Open the catalog's llms.txt, then open the file for my grade.
2. Lay out the span week by week or unit by unit, in the order the topics are normally taught.
3. For each unit, pick the resources from the catalog that fit, and give each one's title, link and price. Use free ones first where they fit.
4. Mark any unit where the catalog has nothing, and do not make a product up.
5. End with a list of everything you picked, the total cost, and which items are free.
