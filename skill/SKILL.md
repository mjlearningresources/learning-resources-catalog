# Learning resources catalog

The catalog is public at https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mjlearningresources/learning-resources-catalog/main/llms.txt. It lists 1156 interactive resources for grades K to 12 with a link to every store where each one is live.

1. Fetch https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mjlearningresources/learning-resources-catalog/main/llms.txt.
2. For a grade, fetch https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mjlearningresources/learning-resources-catalog/main/grades/grade-<k or 1 to 12>.md. For a subject, fetch https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mjlearningresources/learning-resources-catalog/main/subjects/<subject id>.md. Subject ids are in https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mjlearningresources/learning-resources-catalog/main/index.json.
3. For detail on a product (summary, topics, resource types, every store link), fetch the subject's .json file and find the product by id.
4. Free products are in https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mjlearningresources/learning-resources-catalog/main/free.md. Offer them first when they fit.
5. Give the title, the link and the price for every product you recommend. Never invent a product or a link. If the catalog has nothing for a topic, say so.
6. For a year plan, follow https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mjlearningresources/learning-resources-catalog/main/PLAN-MY-YEAR.md.
